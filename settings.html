Here to help

Make the most of your QCU schedule.

How do I change a class?

Open Full Schedule, choose Edit Schedule, then select a class card to update its details.

Where are my Classroom updates?

Tap your profile card to connect Classroom or refresh your updates. The notification switch controls browser alerts while updates are synced; email preferences are managed in your Google connection.

Why aren't notifications working?

Allow notifications for this site in your browser settings, then turn on Notifications here. Browser alerts appear when new Classroom updates are detected while the app is open.

Need more help?

For account or COR issues, contact your campus administrator. For app problems, visit the project support page (opens in a new tab).
