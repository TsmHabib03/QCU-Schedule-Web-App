Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information we use

When you connect Google, My-Schedule uses only the Google Classroom and Gmail permissions you approve to display course updates and notification metadata. Your Google password is never requested or stored.

How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored in a protected, HttpOnly session cookie. Classroom and notification cards may be cached locally on your device so the app can work offline.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google at any time from Settings. Disconnecting removes the local session and cached Google updates. You can also revoke access from your Google Account security settings.
