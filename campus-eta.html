Route 4 · QC Hall ↔ General Luis

QCU San Bartolome

Route 4 runs Mindanao Avenue onto Quirino Highway through Novaliches before turning into General Luis Street. QCU Main Campus is one of its 15 designated stops — the 10th heading to General Luis, the 6th heading back to QC Hall — so both directions serve the campus.
